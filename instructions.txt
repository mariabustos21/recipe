Coca-Cola Chicken

Ingredients:
- Chicken thighs
- Coca-Cola
- Chicken stock
- Onions
- Garlic
- Salt
- Paprika
- Pepper
- Cumin
- Fresh parsley

Instructions:
1. Chop the onions, parsley, and garlic.
2. Season the chicken thighs with the dry spices (salt, paprika, pepper, cumin) and set aside.
3. Cook the onions and garlic until golden brown.
4. Add the chicken thighs and cook until golden.
5. Cover the chicken with Coca-Cola and a little chicken stock for flavor.
6. Let it reduce, making sure the chicken is fully cooked.
7. Finish with fresh parsley.
8. If needed, remove the chicken and let the sauce thicken a bit more before serving.
